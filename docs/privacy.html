Privacy policy

Life Tracker · last updated 18 November 2026

Life Tracker is an Android app that keeps a diary of your day on your phone. We — the developer — run no server and receive none of your data. Nothing described below is sent to us.

What the app keeps, on your phone

  • Location. Your phone's positions while recording is on, used to work out the places you stayed and the journeys between them.
  • Purchases. With notification access you grant, the app reads notifications as they arrive and keeps only those that read as money spent (the app, the words of the notification, the amount and the merchant). From apps you have chosen to count, it also keeps — for seven days, so you can review them — notifications that carry an amount of money but did not read as a purchase. Every other notification is ignored and not stored.
  • Costs you type in, and places you name.
  • Screen time. With usage access you grant, how long each app was on screen each day, and how often the phone was unlocked.

All of this is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

What goes online

  • Map tiles from OpenStreetMap, to draw the map.
  • Place names, unless you turn lookups off in Settings: the coordinates of a spot where you stayed are sent once to OpenStreetMap services (Overpass, Nominatim) and Photon by Komoot, to find what the place is called. No account or identifier is sent with them.
  • Google Drive backup, only if you sign in with Google in Settings (see below).

Google Drive backup

If you sign in with your Google account, Life Tracker asks Google for one permission: drive.file — access to the files this app creates in your Drive, and to nothing else there. Each night it writes your days to My Drive › Life Tracker as plain JSON files: one per day, with that day's positions, stays and journeys, purchases, typed costs and screen time, and one file of your named places. The files go directly from your phone to your Drive; they never pass through any server of ours. Restore reads them back into the phone. Sign out withdraws the app's access; the files stay in your Drive until you delete them.

Life Tracker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from your Drive is used only to restore your own diary to your phone. It is not shared, sold, used for advertising, or read by people.

Deleting your data

  • On your phone: uninstalling Life Tracker deletes everything it recorded. You can also remove a named place, a typed cost or a rule in the app at any time.
  • In your Google Drive: delete the Life Tracker folder in your Drive. Settings › Google account › Sign out withdraws the app's access to it.
  • There is no copy anywhere else: the developer holds none of your data, so there is nothing more to ask for. Questions: mayueanyou@gmail.com.

Sharing

The app shares nothing. If you use Export today or Share diagnostics, the file or text goes only where you send it; the diagnostics report contains no locations.

Children

Life Tracker is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date.
