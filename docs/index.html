Life Recorder

A private diary of your day, kept on your Android phone.

Life Recorder works out where you went and how you got there from your phone's own location, shows your day as a map and a timeline, adds up what you spent from your bank's purchase notifications, and records your screen time. Everything is worked out and kept on the phone. There is no account with us and no server.

If you choose, it backs your days up to your own Google Drive as plain JSON files you can open anywhere. It can see only the files it creates there.
