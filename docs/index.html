Life Tracker

Where does your time go? Where does your money go?

Life Tracker is an Android app that quietly records your days — the places you go, how you get there, what you spend, and how you use your phone — so you can see your life clearly and change what you want to change.

Why

Most of us don't really know where our hours and our money go. Life Tracker keeps the details without you typing anything, so at the end of a day, a week or a month you can look back and see it plainly: the evenings at home, the times you went out, the money spent on restaurants, the hours on your phone. Knowing that is the first step to adjusting your life the way you want — maybe spending less on eating out, maybe getting outside more to stay healthy.

Your data stays yours

  • On your phone, or in your own Google Drive — nowhere else. Backup to your Drive is optional, as plain files you can read yourself.
  • No account, no server, no ads, no tracking. Nothing you record is ever sent to the developer or anyone else.
  • Everything comes from your own phone — its location, the purchase notifications your bank already sends you, and Android's own screen-time record — and is worked out on the phone. From notifications, only those that read as money spent are kept.
  • The little that goes online is listed: map tiles from OpenStreetMap, and, unless you turn it off, the coordinates of a spot sent to OpenStreetMap services to learn its name. Read the privacy policy.
  • Open source (MIT): anyone can check exactly what it does.
